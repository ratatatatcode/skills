# Mobile Application Profile

Inspect these concerns when relevant to the application and its supported platforms.

## Experience and platform behavior

- Supported operating systems, minimum versions, phones/tablets, orientations, screen sizes, and accessibility services.
- First launch, onboarding, permissions, deep links, notifications, background behavior, interruption recovery, and app lifecycle transitions.
- Offline and poor-network behavior, synchronization, conflict resolution, retry, and local data protection.
- Platform conventions, gestures, keyboards, safe areas, localization, time zones, and dark mode.

## Device and data

- Camera, location, biometrics, contacts, storage, Bluetooth, sensors, or other hardware permissions; include denial and revocation behavior.
- Authentication, secure storage, session handling, device change, account recovery, data retention, export, and deletion.
- Battery, memory, startup time, network usage, crash tolerance, and performance thresholds for critical journeys.

## Distribution and operations

- App-store policy constraints, review ownership, privacy disclosures, signing, staged rollout, forced/optional upgrades, and rollback strategy.
- Analytics, crash reporting, consent, remote configuration, feature flags, and support diagnostics.
- Acceptance coverage across supported versions, devices, permission states, connectivity states, and upgrade paths.
