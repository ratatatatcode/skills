# Web Product Profile

Inspect these concerns when they are relevant to the product; do not turn irrelevant checklist items into requirements.

## Experience and access

- Primary journeys, navigation, information architecture, empty/loading/error states, and recovery paths.
- Responsive behavior and supported viewport classes.
- Accessibility target, keyboard operation, focus behavior, semantic structure, contrast, and assistive-technology expectations.
- Browser and device support with an explicit support policy.
- Authentication, authorization, session expiry, account recovery, and privileged operations.

## System behavior

- Client/server boundaries, APIs, background jobs, third-party services, webhooks, and failure handling.
- Data ownership, validation, retention, export, deletion, consent, and auditability.
- Performance measures for critical journeys, capacity assumptions, caching, and degraded behavior.
- Search, localization, time zones, notifications, analytics, SEO, and content management when applicable.

## Delivery and operations

- Environments, configuration, feature flags, deployment constraints, rollback, observability, support, and incident ownership.
- Security and privacy threats appropriate to the data and exposed surface.
- Acceptance coverage across roles, browsers, responsive layouts, integration failures, and accessibility.
