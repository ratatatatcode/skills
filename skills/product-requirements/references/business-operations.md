# Business Operations Profile

Model the operating process, not just a proposed interface.

## Process and ownership

- Trigger, inputs, outputs, completion conditions, actors, accountable owner, handoffs, and service expectations.
- Current-state pain points and target-state changes; separate observed problems from proposed solutions.
- Decision rules, approval thresholds, segregation of duties, exception paths, escalation, and rework.
- Volumes, seasonality, deadlines, queues, capacity constraints, and continuity requirements.

## Information and controls

- Systems of record, data owners, required fields, validation, reconciliation, retention, deletion, access, and audit trail.
- Policies, contracts, regulations, evidence requirements, and control owners.
- Integrations, imports/exports, manual workarounds, duplicate entry, and failure recovery.
- Reports, dashboards, recipients, cadence, calculation definitions, and decision use.

## Outcomes and adoption

- Baseline and target measures for time, cost, quality, risk, customer outcome, or employee effort.
- Training, communications, role changes, rollout, support, and adoption measures.
- Acceptance scenarios for standard cases, exceptions, rejected approvals, missing data, unavailable systems, and audit requests.
